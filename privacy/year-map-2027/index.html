The service has no sign-up and no accounts. The birth date, optional birth hour and gender you enter are used to compute your chart inside your own browser. We do not send them to our server.

In the apps, the daily reminder is scheduled on your device and nothing about it is sent to us. Share cards go through your phone's share sheet to the app you pick.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, we show no ads, and we do not sell personal data. Payment is not open yet; no payment data is processed.

3. AI-generated content

The map is computed by a fixed calendar engine and a fixed scoring rule applied the same way to everyone. The wording and the character artwork are AI-generated entertainment content. It describes tendencies, does not predict events, and does not replace professional medical, legal or financial advice.

4. Your choices

  • Delete your data — clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your birth date and diary are only stored there.
  • Analytics opt-out — install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or block analytics in your browser.

5. Children

The service is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
