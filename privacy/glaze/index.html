Privacy Policy

Glaze (Android app) · Last updated November 11, 2026

한국어 ↓

Glaze has no sign-up and no accounts. Photos and videos you take stay on your phone. The only data that leaves it is anonymous usage statistics, listed below.

1. What we process

DataPurpose · processorKept
Photos and videos you take, and edited copies Saved to Pictures/Glaze and Movies/Glaze on your phone. Scene recognition, best-shot scoring (sharpness, exposure, eyes open, smile) and edits all run on your phone. Never uploaded. On your phone until you delete them
Anonymous usage events (scene recognized, guide opened, shot taken, edit saved, share tapped), app version, device model, OS version, country/region (from IP address), random app-instance ID See which features are used and whether people come back. Google Analytics for Firebase — Google LLC. No advertising ID is collected. Up to 14 months in Google Analytics
Daily count of comparison shots Stored only on your phone Removed when you uninstall

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos.
  • Microphone — to record sound when you shoot video.
  • Glaze does not ask for access to your photo library. It only reads the files it saved itself.

3. Sharing to Instagram

When you tap "Share to Instagram", your phone hands the chosen photo to the Instagram app through Android's share function. We do not receive it. What Instagram does with it is covered by Instagram's own policy.

4. Third parties

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics. Google privacy policy

5. Your choices

  • Local data — uninstalling the app removes the app's settings. Photos you took stay in your gallery until you delete them.
  • Consent — usage statistics are switched off until you answer the prompt on first launch. The legal basis is your consent. You can change your answer at any time in the app's settings; turning it off stops collection from then on.
  • Your rights — wherever you live, you may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data about you, and you may withdraw consent. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK you may also object to processing and complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Deletion request — usage statistics are tied only to a random ID. Email us and we will help you request deletion where possible.
  • International processing — Google may process usage statistics on servers outside your country, including the United States. Controller: Hundred Core (osu355@gmail.com).

6. Children

Glaze is intended for people aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: osu355@gmail.com · Hundred Core
