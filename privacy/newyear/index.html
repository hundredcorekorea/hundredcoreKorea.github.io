Privacy Policy

2027 New Year Reading · 2027 신년운세 (Android app, com.hundredcore.newyear) · Last updated October 23, 2026

한국어 ↓

The app has no sign-up and no accounts. To compute your reading it sends the birth date, birth hour (optional) and gender you enter to our server. Nothing else from your device is sent by us.

1. What we process

DataPurpose · processorKept
Birth date, birth hour (optional), gender you enter Compute your four pillars and the 2027 year/months; the computed values are sent to an AI model that writes the reading — Vercel (server), OpenRouter (AI) Not stored as entered
Your reading (computed chart values and the AI-written text) Saved so a reading you unlocked can be opened again — Supabase Until you ask us to delete it
One-way hash of your IP address Daily usage limit to prevent abuse. Your IP address itself is not stored — Supabase Until you ask us to delete it
Google Play purchase token and order ID Verify a purchase with Google before unlocking the detailed reading — Google Play, Supabase Until you ask us to delete it
Advertising ID (AAID), device and network info, IP address Rewarded ads you choose to watch, and invalid-click protection — Google AdMob Per Google's policy

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Third parties

  • Vercel — server functions. Privacy policy
  • OpenRouter — routes the computed values to the AI model that writes the reading. Privacy policy
  • Supabase — stores readings. Privacy policy
  • Google Play — in-app purchases. Google AdMob — rewarded ads. How Google uses data

3. AI-generated content

The four pillars and monthly scores are computed by a fixed calendar engine; the text is generated by AI for entertainment only. It does not replace professional medical, legal or financial advice. Illustrations are AI-generated. You can report an inappropriate result from inside the app.

4. Your choices

  • Deletion — email us and we will delete stored readings and purchase records tied to your request.
  • Ads personalization — Android Settings › Privacy › Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
